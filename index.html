01Direction

ACiD/iCE-style ANSI group logos and boxed .NFO release tables, CRT/VHS chromatic-aberration glitch text, 80s–90s arcade cabinet neon geometry, phone-phreaking iconography as a texture accent. Avoid corporate SaaS gradients, soft rounded cards, and any "clean flat design" pass that sands off the CRT/ANSI texture — the texture is the brand.

05Two treatment modes

Full color everywhere doesn't survive a printer or a client's inbox — same brand, same voice, one leans into color/glow, the other leans harder on ASCII structure.

DIGITAL — full treatment

  • Website, resume site, PowerPoint, merch, social
  • Full Arcade New Wave palette
  • Full glitch / chromatic-offset lockup
  • Neon glow, scanlines, drop-shadow texture

PRINT / CORPORATE — B&W

  • Word docs, PDF reports, letterhead, email
  • Pure black on white — no color, no glow
  • Flat monochrome mark, plain bold monospace wordmark
  • ASCII rules (====), bracket fields, :: / ~/ notation carry the identity instead
