Error404 Mono (work in progress)

LICENSING
  The font files in this folder (*.ttf, *.otf, *.woff2) are licensed under the SIL Open Font
  License 1.1 (see OFL.txt), because they are derived from two OFL fonts. The OFL requires
  derivatives to stay under the OFL, so the font cannot be relicensed as MIT.
  The build and check scripts in this folder (*.py) and the rest of this repository are MIT
  (see ../LICENSE).

SOURCES
  Capitals, digits and punctuation: Share Tech Mono (Carrois Type Design, Ralph du Carrois), OFL 1.1.
  Lowercase, symbols and arrows: JetBrains Mono (The JetBrains Mono Project Authors), OFL 1.1,
  rescaled to a 540-unit advance and 700-unit cap height.
  Drawn here (original): 0 and Ø (zero_glyphs.py), O (round_glyphs.py), the constellation
  icon at U+E404 (icon_glyph.py).
  The font is renamed (Error404 Mono) and does not use either donor's name.

BUILD
  Full build:  python3 build_font.py out.ttf   (donor TTFs are read from /tmp/fonts; edit the two
               paths at the top of build_font.py)
  Patch only:  python3 patch_glyphs.py IN.ttf OUT.ttf   (swaps in the house-drawn glyphs)
  Needs: fontTools, skia-pathops
