Error404 Mono v0.1 (work in progress)

Hybrid monospaced font. Capitals, digits and punctuation come from Share Tech Mono
(Ralph du Carrois). Lowercase, symbols and arrows come from JetBrains Mono
(The JetBrains Mono Project Authors), rescaled to a 540-unit advance and 700-unit cap height.
Zero (U+0030) and O-slash (U+00D8) are drawn here: the slash runs at the angle of the
constellation edge (204.0,74.9) -> (80.2,202.0) = 45.75 deg.

Both donor fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
